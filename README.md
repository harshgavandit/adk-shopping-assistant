# ADK Shopping

ADK Shopping is a chat-based shopping demo. You can browse a small electronics catalog, choose a product, add it to your cart and get an order summary.

[Open the app](https://adk-shopping-assistant.vercel.app)

## How it works

1. Start a chat and provide a name, email address and mobile number. You can use made-up details to try the app.
2. Browse smartphones, laptops or headphones and ask about the available products and prices.
3. Choose a product and quantity. The app checks the selection and calculates the total.
4. Continue to checkout, enter a shipping address and ask for your order summary.

The cart holds one product at a time, with a quantity of up to 99. Choosing a different product replaces the current selection. Your chat and cart remain available in the same browser for 24 hours after your last completed message. Use **New chat** to start again.

This is a demo catalog with fixed prices. It does not check live stock, collect payments, place real orders or track deliveries.
